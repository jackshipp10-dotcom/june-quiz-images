THREE JOBS THIS MONTH

Plant your garlic. Get every clove in this month. It needs the cold to split into a bulb.

Net your kale and sprouts. The pigeons get hungry once the weather turns, and they'll strip a plant in a morning.

Check bonfire piles before you light them. Hedgehogs love a heap of sticks. Move it to a fresh spot, or check it with a torch first.

SOW, PLANT AND PICK

Sow: garlic, a hardy broad bean like Aquadulce, autumn onion sets, and a tray of pea shoots on the windowsill.

Plant: bare root fruit bushes and rhubarb crowns. They're cheapest now and settle in well over winter.

Pick: leeks, parsnips, kale, sprouts, cabbage, chard, celeriac and swede.

Leave your parsnips in until after the first frost. The cold turns them sweet.

COOK IT
Honey roast parsnips
Serves 4. 750g parsnips, 2 tbsp oil, 1 tbsp honey, a few sprigs of thyme, salt and pepper.

1. Cut the parsnips into long wedges and toss with the oil, salt and pepper.

2. Roast at 200°C (180°C fan) for 30 minutes, turning once.

3. Drizzle over the honey, add the thyme and roast 10 minutes more until sticky.
