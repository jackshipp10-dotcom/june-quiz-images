Privacy

Last updated 31 October 2026.

Who runs this site

Plot to Plate with June is run by Jack Shipp, Putney Bridge Road, London, United Kingdom. You can contact us at june@plottoplatejune.co.uk.

What we collect

The quiz. If you take the free quiz, your answers and, if you choose to give it, your name and email are collected by Tally (our form provider). If you tick the box to hear from us, your email, name and quiz answers are added to our mailing list.

The calendar by email. If you sign up for the monthly calendar, your email is added to our mailing list.

Mailing list. Our emails are sent with MailerLite. We use your details only to send you the emails you asked for, such as your quiz result, growing tips and the monthly calendar. Every email has an unsubscribe link, and you can ask us to delete your details at any time.

Buying a planner. Purchases are handled by Payhip, which processes your payment and email to deliver your download. Pages with a Buy button load Payhip’s checkout script, which may set its own cookies to run the checkout. We don’t see or store your card details.

Visits to this site. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits. It doesn’t use cookies or track you across other sites. This site sets no cookies of its own.

Why we can use your details

We email you only because you asked us to (your consent). You can withdraw it whenever you like by unsubscribing.

How long we keep them

Until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete them.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the details we hold about you by emailing june@plottoplatejune.co.uk. If you’re unhappy with how we’ve handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
