Keep track of every packet, swap the spares, and label what you sow.

My seeds live in an old biscuit tin, filed by the month I sow them. On the first of each month I open it, pull out that month’s cards, and I know exactly what’s going in. This kit is everything you need to do the same.

What’s inside (12 pages)

  • Seed tin inventory: every packet you own, with its sow by date
  • Blank seed cards to fill in, one per crop, with space for sowing, spacing and notes
  • 12 month dividers with staggered tabs, so every month shows, and what’s usually sown or planted that month
  • A fold up seed swap envelope
  • My swap list: what you have and what you’re after
  • Plant labels for 32 crops, plus a page of blank ones
  • Sowing record: what you sowed and how many came up

Formats

  • A4 and US Letter PDFs
  • Print on card (160 to 200 gsm) for the cards, dividers and labels
  • Print as many as you need for your own garden
