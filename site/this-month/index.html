Cook it

Honey roast parsnips

Serves 4

You’ll need

  • 750g parsnips
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • A few sprigs of thyme
  • Salt and pepper

Method

  1. Cut the parsnips into long wedges and toss with the oil, salt and pepper.
  2. Roast at 200°C (180°C fan) for 30 minutes, turning once.
  3. Drizzle over the honey, add the thyme and roast 10 minutes more until sticky.

Jobs to tick off

  • Plant garlic
  • Net kale and sprouts against pigeons
  • Mulch empty beds
  • Bag up fallen leaves for leaf mould
  • Lift pots onto bricks
  • Clean and oil your tools
  • Check bonfire piles for hedgehogs before lighting
